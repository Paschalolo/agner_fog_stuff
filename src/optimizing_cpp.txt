Instruction Set architectures 
CISC - complex instruction set computer i.e x86
RISC - Reduced instruction set Architecture i.e ARM , RISC-v 

CISC tend to have reduce insturctions to perform an operation . all modern isa tend to execute on out of order superscalar processors so instruction are broken down into micro ops to exploit this hence 
	e.g 
		call printf 
		broken down into 
		sub rsp , 8 
		mov [rsp] , rip
		jmp printf_offset 
 CISC has had addition of more registers with the introduction of AVX512

 8 mask regiters k0 - k7
 vector registers zmm0- zmm31
 16 General Purpose registers rax - r15
